Your server, your data

The website

This static website stores appearance preferences locally. Optional GoatCounter analytics are enabled only when the site operator configures an endpoint. It does not collect account passwords or remote screen content.

The self-hosted service

Your server stores account, device, session and tunnel metadata and handles authentication and signaling. Remote screen and input use authenticated encrypted direct P2P. FRP tunnel traffic follows the deployment and access policies configured by your administrator.

Control your access

Sign out to close remote sessions. Revoke a device to remove its credentials and stop its services. Ask your own service administrator about retention and deletion.
